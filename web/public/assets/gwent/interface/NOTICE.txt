AI Gwent Arena interface

The original interface, card content, selected illustrations and thumbnails are
included with permission from the rights holder. The integrated interface uses
the original layouts, deck editor, card catalog and local demonstration engine.
Sound effects are synthesized locally. Online matches use the platform server.

The original interface identifies Gwent Classic as its underlying game engine:
https://github.com/asundr/gwent-classic
