# The Gacha Simulator assets

The card illustrations, theme group illustrations, pool covers and animation layers are original project artwork derived from the project's established AI character designs. The activity contains 64 cards, 19 theme illustrations, thirteen covers, fourteen site backgrounds and animation layers for 17 SSR and seven SP cards. Illustration production used generative image tools with character, costume and composition references. These project assets are distributed under AGPL-3.0, to the extent rights apply.

High-resolution WebP derivatives retain the established compositions and use image enhancement for large displays. They do not represent additional native source detail. The site wallpapers include independently composited characters, eye frames, scenery, clouds, celestial bodies and a travelling whale. The celestial and wandering static backgrounds are composited from those same layers, including a complete shaded lunar disk in the celestial scenes. The corresponding portraits and eye frames include localized hand-anatomy repairs. Eye-contour coordinates are derived from the corresponding painted open and closed portraits. Registered architecture, ornament and cloud masks preserve depth in the source painting; these masks supply opacity while the original scene supplies colour and detail.

All cards now have retained background and foreground inputs. Forty ordinary card foregrounds, nineteen character-free theme backgrounds, and the registered foregrounds and plates for group illustrations and covers were derived from the established artwork. `composition.json` records source-over composition and retained rare-card layers. Native generated portraits are 1024×1536; native landscape plates are approximately 1672×941. Larger derivatives are resampled from these inputs and do not add native detail.

Seven ordinary foregrounds retain generative corrections of their established portraits: `a-chatgpt` pours into a cup on a table; `c-deepseek` touches a visible puddle; `d-chatgpt` supports her saucer; `e-claude` and `e-glm` retain their tables; and `f-chatgpt` and `f-claude` retain their seat and trunk. Their character designs, theme backgrounds and composition registration are preserved. Each revised 1024 × 1536 transparent source supplies its standard card, 256 × 384 thumbnail, foreground and 2560 × 3840 resampled derivatives. Independent group illustrations and covers retain their own compositions.

The permanent-pool cover retains a localized generative correction of ChatGPT's outstretched hand to five fingers. Only the hand region of the generated 1672 × 941 foreground edit is adopted; the remaining character artwork, background and composition registration are preserved. Both transparent foregrounds and composited covers share the correction at standard size and 3840 × 2160 resampled size.

ChatGPT, Claude, GLM, DeepSeek and Gemini names identify the fictional character inspirations. Related names and marks belong to their respective holders. The activity is not affiliated with or endorsed by those holders.

The soundtrack `audio/lobby.mp3` is the project's lobby arrangement of **Elevate** by **Fupi**, based on the [Elevate instrument tracks](https://opengameart.org/content/elevate-instrument-tracks), released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The source composition and instrument tracks retain that dedication.

The nine short activity sound effects are synthesized by the project's original Web Audio score. Their implementation is distributed under AGPL-3.0 and contains no third-party sound recordings.

Native scenery is 1672 × 941; the character and eye plates are 1024 × 1536. The portrait, independent cover, wide illustration and responsive appearances share these layers, individually measured eyelids and localized hair, coat and foliage motion. Standard portraits are 1280 × 1920; standard landscapes are 1920 × 1080. Their 2560 × 3840 and 3840 × 2160 derivatives are resampled, not additional native detail. All of this theme's artwork is non-explicit. No encounter video or additional audio is included.

The cloud companion retains its native aspect ratio. This portrait study has separate static and large resampled exports; the first version and other formats remain available for comparison.

## Moment-only expanded frames

The adopted derivatives in `moment/c-gemini/`, `moment/q-glm/`, `moment/n-gemini/` and
`moment/o-claude/`, with matching `hires/moment/` paths, retain the established
characters and original card and appearance inputs. Their separate
`composition.json` registrations describe the expanded canvas and the unchanged
original region. Current standard frames are 1536 × 1536; 3840 × 3840 derivatives
use 2.5× Lanczos resampling and do not add native detail.

c-gemini's additional margins were generated from its retained project source
using [BRIA Fibo-2.0-edit-turbo](https://huggingface.co/briaai/Fibo-2.0-edit-turbo/tree/177f2d4a06b6587144ef5c2b8452a4f1fe2ab4b7),
with foreground alpha prepared using
[ToonOut](https://huggingface.co/joelseytre/toonout/tree/cbf720eca394edcde66b861a8a8c20fbabe9c748).
The complete original RGBA rectangle, including its forward hand, remains
unchanged. The three native SP derivatives instead reveal the existing
uncropped animation inputs through an expanded camera; no new character parts
were generated. n-gemini's static original region was restored from the same
native render baseline. Their native rigs, eye coordinates and motion remain
the original inputs.

The authoring models and inference code are not bundled with these web assets.
The retained Fibo model card identifies the model's
[CC BY-NC 4.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/deed.en)
and links a separate
[BRIA commercial agreement](https://platform.bria.ai/licensing/pricing?model=fibo-2).
Those model terms are distinct from this project's artwork and code notices.
The pinned source and processing records retain model revisions, source and
output hashes, alpha checks and adoption status.
