# The Gacha Simulator assets

The card illustrations, theme group illustrations, pool covers and animation layers are original project artwork derived from the project's established AI character designs. The activity contains 55 cards, 11 theme illustrations, five covers and animation layers for 15 SSR cards. Illustration production used generative image tools with character, costume and composition references. These project assets are distributed under AGPL-3.0, to the extent rights apply.

ChatGPT, Claude, GLM, DeepSeek and Gemini names identify the fictional character inspirations. Related names and marks belong to their respective holders. The activity is not affiliated with or endorsed by those holders.

The soundtrack `audio/lobby.mp3` is the project's lobby arrangement of **Elevate** by **Fupi**, based on the [Elevate instrument tracks](https://opengameart.org/content/elevate-instrument-tracks), released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The source composition and instrument tracks retain that dedication.

The eight short activity sound effects are synthesized by the project's original Web Audio score. Their implementation is distributed under AGPL-3.0 and contains no third-party sound recordings.
