# The Gacha Simulator assets

The card illustrations, theme group illustrations, pool covers and animation layers are original project artwork derived from the project's established AI character designs. The activity contains 57 cards, 13 theme illustrations, seven covers, four site backgrounds and animation layers for 15 SSR and two SP cards. Illustration production used generative image tools with character, costume and composition references. These project assets are distributed under AGPL-3.0, to the extent rights apply.

High-resolution WebP derivatives retain the established compositions and use image enhancement for large displays. They do not represent additional native source detail. The site wallpapers include independently composited characters, eye frames, scenery, clouds, celestial bodies and a travelling whale. All four static backgrounds are composited from those same layers, including a complete shaded lunar disk in the celestial scenes. The corresponding portraits and eye frames include localized hand-anatomy repairs. Eye-contour coordinates are derived from the corresponding painted open and closed portraits. Registered architecture, ornament and cloud masks preserve depth in the source painting; these masks supply opacity while the original scene supplies colour and detail.

ChatGPT, Claude, GLM, DeepSeek and Gemini names identify the fictional character inspirations. Related names and marks belong to their respective holders. The activity is not affiliated with or endorsed by those holders.

The soundtrack `audio/lobby.mp3` is the project's lobby arrangement of **Elevate** by **Fupi**, based on the [Elevate instrument tracks](https://opengameart.org/content/elevate-instrument-tracks), released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The source composition and instrument tracks retain that dedication.

The nine short activity sound effects are synthesized by the project's original Web Audio score. Their implementation is distributed under AGPL-3.0 and contains no third-party sound recordings.
