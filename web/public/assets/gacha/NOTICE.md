# The Gacha Simulator assets

The card illustrations, theme group illustrations, pool covers and animation layers are original project artwork derived from the project's established AI character designs. The activity contains 61 cards, 17 theme illustrations, eleven covers, twelve site backgrounds and animation layers for 15 SSR and six SP cards. Illustration production used generative image tools with character, costume and composition references. These project assets are distributed under AGPL-3.0, to the extent rights apply.

High-resolution WebP derivatives retain the established compositions and use image enhancement for large displays. They do not represent additional native source detail. The site wallpapers include independently composited characters, eye frames, scenery, clouds, celestial bodies and a travelling whale. The celestial and wandering static backgrounds are composited from those same layers, including a complete shaded lunar disk in the celestial scenes. The corresponding portraits and eye frames include localized hand-anatomy repairs. Eye-contour coordinates are derived from the corresponding painted open and closed portraits. Registered architecture, ornament and cloud masks preserve depth in the source painting; these masks supply opacity while the original scene supplies colour and detail.

All cards now have retained background and foreground inputs. Forty ordinary card foregrounds, seventeen character-free theme backgrounds, and the registered foregrounds and plates for group illustrations and covers were derived from the established artwork. `composition.json` records source-over composition and retained rare-card layers. Native generated portraits are 1024×1536; native landscape plates are approximately 1672×941. Larger derivatives are resampled from these inputs and do not add native detail.

ChatGPT, Claude, GLM, DeepSeek and Gemini names identify the fictional character inspirations. Related names and marks belong to their respective holders. The activity is not affiliated with or endorsed by those holders.

The soundtrack `audio/lobby.mp3` is the project's lobby arrangement of **Elevate** by **Fupi**, based on the [Elevate instrument tracks](https://opengameart.org/content/elevate-instrument-tracks), released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The source composition and instrument tracks retain that dedication.

The nine short activity sound effects are synthesized by the project's original Web Audio score. Their implementation is distributed under AGPL-3.0 and contains no third-party sound recordings.

The four additional SP scenes retain registered backgrounds, transparent portraits, closed-eye or articulated character inputs, and their accepted portrait crop. Their static card, cover, illustration and light/dark appearance derivatives share those inputs. Heartfelt-moment layers are rendered from the same scene at time zero; the background remains static and only the character animates. No encounter video or additional background music is included.

Content note: the adult Gemini in Across the Mirror Sea wears an off-shoulder gown with a high slit; the adult Claude in Run Away with You wears an off-shoulder cropped blouse and shorts; the adult GLM in A Notice to Steal Your Heart wears a fitted outfit with a deep neckline and waist openings. These non-explicit romantic illustrations appear in the cards, covers, theme illustrations and wallpapers. Deployers can choose whether to use these themes. Amber Vow uses ceremonial robes and armor.
