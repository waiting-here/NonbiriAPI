# Game audio

The Likes Battle music and defeat sting are arrangements of
[Elevate (+ instrument tracks)](https://opengameart.org/content/elevate-instrument-tracks),
published under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The arrangements and the original synthesized game sound effects are distributed
under this repository's AGPL-3.0 license. No external recordings, sound fonts,
voices, or runtime media services are included.

The six music loops have 2,919,724 frames at 44,100 Hz, stereo, with 192 beats
per loop. The separate defeat sting lasts 3.2 seconds. FLAC preserves the
24-bit masters exactly; the lightweight files use 192 kbps MP3 with gapless
metadata. Short effects use 16-bit PCM WAV. Music and effects are loaded only
after the corresponding browser preference is enabled.

`manifest.json` records the master and distributed file hashes and their uses.
