# Game audio

The Turn-based Battle Minigame (Test) music and defeat sting are arrangements of
[Elevate (+ instrument tracks)](https://opengameart.org/content/elevate-instrument-tracks)
by Fupi, published under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The CC0 grant covers the original source work and permits modification and redistribution;
it is not an AGPL relicensing of that source. The six project loops and defeat arrangement,
including project edits and packaging, are distributed under this repository's AGPL-3.0
terms. The original synthesized game sound effects are independent project works under
AGPL-3.0. No additional sampled libraries, sound fonts, voices, or runtime media services are included.

The six music loops have 2,919,724 frames at 44,100 Hz, stereo, with 192 beats
per loop. The separate defeat sting lasts 3.2 seconds. FLAC preserves the
24-bit masters exactly; the lightweight files use 192 kbps MP3 with gapless
metadata. Short effects use 16-bit PCM WAV. Music and effects are loaded only
after the corresponding browser preference is enabled.

`manifest.json` records the master and distributed file hashes and their uses.
